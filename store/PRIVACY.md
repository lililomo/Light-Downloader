# Privacy policy — Light Downloader Integration

_Last updated: 2026-10-29_

Hosted at **https://beliaja.id/light-downloader/privacy/**. Paste that URL into the
listing's privacy-policy field; keep this file and the hosted page in sync.

## What the extension accesses
For a download you choose to capture (by starting it, or via the right-click
menu), the extension reads:
- the download URL,
- the originating site's cookies,
- the page referrer and the browser user-agent.

## How it is used
This information is sent **only** to the Light Downloader desktop application
running on your own computer, over a local loopback connection
(`http://127.0.0.1`). It is used solely to perform the download you requested.

## What it does NOT do
- It does not send any data to the developer or any third-party/remote server.
- It does not track your browsing, read page content, or build any profile.
- It stores nothing except a single on/off preference in the browser's local
  storage.
- It contains no analytics and no remote code.

## Control
Turn automatic capture off at any time from the extension popup, or remove the
extension. When the desktop app is not running, nothing is captured and downloads
proceed normally in the browser.
